# Waitlist community logos

Local brand marks used to identify waitlist members' employers and institutions.
They describe the community, not company partnerships or endorsements.

- Cisco: https://svgl.app/library/cisco_light.svg
- Google: https://svgl.app/library/google-wordmark.svg
- MongoDB: https://svgl.app/library/mongodb-wordmark-dark.svg
- Salesforce: https://svgl.app/library/salesforce.svg (cloud and embedded wordmark)
- Amazon: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_2024.svg
- Autodesk: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Autodesk_Logo_2021.svg
- GovAI (Centre for the Governance of AI): white logo from https://www.governance.ai
- BMO: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BMO_Logo.svg
- University of Toronto: https://chang.eeb.utoronto.ca/uoft_logo-svg/

Original colors are preserved. Dark Autodesk and University of Toronto marks
use gray for contrast; Amazon's dark lettering is gray while its smile stays orange.
